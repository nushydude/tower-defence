Limits concurrent active turrets on the field. Upgrades do not consume cap, incentivizing deep tech evolution over spam.
🪙 Duplicate Purchase Surcharge
Each additional tower of the same root branch costs more, penalizing mono-spamming and rewarding diverse weapon combinations.

Heavy enemies reduce damage per hit flat. Bounces weak rapid-fire bullets (1 dmg); heavy Snipers, Railguns, and Shotguns punch through.

Fast and elite creeps gain blue energy barriers. Normal bullets chip slowly, while Laser Railguns and Quantum Energy towers deal 200% bonus damage!

Places glowing tactical pedestals on the map. Towers stationed on them gain +25% Range, +15% Damage, and ignore 50% enemy armor.
